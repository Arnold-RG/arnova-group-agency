# ArnovaGroup

**Building Skills. Creating Opportunities. Powering Businesses.**

ArnovaGroup is a Rwanda-focused digital services and career-support brand. We help people and small businesses with websites, mobile/web apps, business dashboards, data collection and analysis, information research, job application preparation and student technical project mentorship.

## Services
- Websites, business directories, online catalogues and booking sites
- Small-business apps and inventory, sales and appointment dashboards
- CV review, vacancy research, application support and interview preparation (the applicant approves submissions)
- Data collection, data cleaning, Excel dashboards and research reporting
- Student learning projects in programming, Linux, Docker, Kubernetes and cybersecurity (academic integrity required)

## Values
Ambition, Reliability, New Ideas, Opportunities, Value and Advancement.

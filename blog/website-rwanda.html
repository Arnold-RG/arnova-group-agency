Does your business in Rwanda need a website?

A useful website makes it easier for customers to understand what you offer and how to reach you. The key is choosing the right scope.

Social media, phone calls and Google Maps listings are valuable, but they serve different roles. A website gives your business one clear place to explain services, hours, location, pricing information where appropriate, and booking or ordering steps.

Start with customer questions

What do people ask before visiting? Which products or services are most important? Can customers find your contact details quickly on mobile? Answer these before choosing colors or animations.

Useful features for local businesses

  • Salons and barbers: service lists, location, appointment requests and clear contact details.
  • Supermarkets and shops: mobile product catalogue, ordering workflow and opening hours.
  • Restaurants: up-to-date menu, directions, reservations or enquiries.
  • Professional services: evidence of work, enquiry options and a concise explanation of what is provided.

Keep the first version realistic

A small, well-designed website with accurate information is better than a complicated product that is never maintained. Consider accessibility, performance, ownership of accounts, security and transparent ongoing costs.

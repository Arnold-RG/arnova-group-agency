SMALL BUSINESS · PRACTICAL GUIDE

Does your small business really need a website?

If customers already find you through WhatsApp, Instagram or Google Maps, here's how to decide whether a website would help.

Imagine someone hears about your barbershop, salon or supermarket from a friend. They look you up on their phone. Can they find your opening hours, prices, location and a simple way to contact you? If the answer is no, a small website may make their next step easier.

You don't need a giant website

For many local businesses, a clear one-page site is a good start. It can answer the questions customers ask most often: what do you offer, where are you, when are you open, and how do they reach you?

What should be on the first page?

  • Your real services: a clear list of what people can book or buy.
  • Contact details: a working phone number or WhatsApp link.
  • Location: your correct address and an accurate map link.
  • Photos: pictures of your actual work or premises that you have permission to use.
  • One clear next step: contact, book, enquire or visit.

What about Google Maps?

Your Google Business Profile can help customers discover your location. A website gives them a place to see more detail. Both are useful, but neither guarantees that customers will buy from you.

Keep the business in control

Before launching, agree on the cost, who owns the domain, who updates prices and what happens if you need changes later. A good website should be easy to maintain.

## Publishing boundaries
- Website examples contain **no business-owned Google Maps images, official logos, reviews, product prices, contact forms or booking/order claims**.
- Any depicted categories or products are **illustrative design placeholders**, not confirmed stock, official menu items, offers or services.
- Every page has an immediately visible "UNOFFICIAL" notice and `noindex,nofollow`.
- Links take visitors to the real map listings or the ArnovaGroup contact page to discuss website design, not to fake order flows.
- A proposal must receive the owner's approval, real media rights, actual inventory/menu, confirmed operating details, and agreed domain/hosting before turning into an official client website.

The concepts are a sales portfolio and a proposed creative direction, **not client contracts**.

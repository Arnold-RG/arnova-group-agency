What happens after the first year?

A website purchase is not the same as paying for a domain forever. Your first year includes eligible .co.rw domain and static hosting; keeping the site online requires renewal after that.

Domain choice First-year domain upgrade Estimated yearly renewal with static hosting administration
.co.rw Included 18,000 RWF (12,000 domain + 6,000 hosting administration)
.rw +9,000 RWF Confirm at quotation
.com +23,000 RWF 38,000 RWF (32,000 domain + 6,000 hosting administration)
.net +24,000 RWF Confirm at quotation
.org +23,000 RWF Confirm at quotation

These are indicative prices, not live domain availability or a guaranteed registrar quote. Domain taxes, provider charges and non-static hosting requirements may change the final price.

Optional care and edits

Basic maintenance: 8,000 RWF/month. Business maintenance: 15,000 RWF/month. Both are optional; scope is confirmed in your quote.

Small edit: from 3,000 RWF. Extra page: from 10,000 RWF. Annual refresh: from 20,000 RWF. Full redesigns and new features are quoted separately.

Domain/hosting renewal is separate from optional maintenance unless expressly bundled in your quote.

A one-time website payment starts from 35,000 RWF. Yearly renewal for an eligible .co.rw domain plus static hosting administration starts from 18,000 RWF per year. A .com renewal with hosting administration is estimated at 38,000 RWF/year. These renewals are not a new website-build fee.

Frequently asked questions

Can you build a website for a small business in Rwanda?

Yes. We can discuss a clear, mobile-friendly website sized to your business needs and budget.

We can design a WhatsApp enquiry flow where it suits your business. Messaging requires a working number and customer consent.

These can be scoped where suitable, but payment and booking integrations depend on the providers, requirements and approvals involved.

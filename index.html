QUESTIONS YOU MAY HAVE

Good to know before we start.

No need to guess how it works. Here are the answers to a few common questions.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need — a small business page is different from a catalogue, booking system or custom application. Tell us your goal and we'll explain the options and provide a clear quote before paid work starts.

I don't know anything about websites. Can you still help?

Absolutely. You don't need technical knowledge. Tell us what your customers should be able to do, and we'll explain the rest without confusing jargon.

Can you guarantee I'll get a job?

No service can guarantee an employer's decision. We can help you improve your CV, find suitable listings, prepare applications and practice interviews. You approve any application before it is sent.

Can you complete my school assignment for me?

We can tutor, help you choose a project, explain code and offer learning examples. Students remain responsible for their own academic work and must follow their school's rules.

How will we communicate?

Start on WhatsApp. We can discuss the project, share the scope and agree on the next steps there. A custom company email inbox will be added once it is verified.

Choose a payment plan that fits your business.

These are three different ways to purchase a website. You choose one plan for the build; maintenance is optional. The total price and payment dates are always written in your quotation.

Option 1 · One-time

Pay once. Own your website.

Pay the agreed website package price before work begins. The Starter package starts at 35,000 RWF and includes an eligible first-year .co.rw domain and static hosting.

After year one: Pay the disclosed domain and hosting renewal to keep the website online. Extra edits are charged only if requested.

Option 2 · Staged payment

Split an approved project invoice.

For eligible projects, request a written two-stage schedule, for example 50% to begin and 50% before launch. This does not reduce the agreed total.

Example: A 65,000 RWF Business website would be 32,500 RWF to begin and 32,500 RWF before launch, if approved. Domain purchase timing and cancellation terms are agreed in advance.

Option 3 · Monthly subscription

Spread the website cost.

A proposed Starter subscription is 12,000 RWF per month under a 12-month minimum agreement (144,000 RWF over the full term if all payments are made).

Before subscribing: We agree on maintenance scope, renewal, late payments, cancellation, website ownership and transfer rights. This is an alternative to buying upfront, not an extra mandatory fee.

No hidden compulsory maintenance: Buying a website once does not require monthly care. Annual domain and hosting renewals are separate. If you need a new page, new feature, annual refresh or ongoing updates, choose only what you need.

Product Catalog — from 120,000 RWF

Questions before you order

Is the 35,000 RWF website payment really one-time?

Yes, the website build is a one-time payment for the agreed scope. It includes the first year of an eligible .co.rw domain and static hosting. Domain and hosting renew annually afterward.

Do I have to pay monthly maintenance?

No. Monthly maintenance is optional. You can request individual edits when needed. Annual domain and hosting renewal still applies.

Who owns the domain?

The domain should be registered in the customer's legal name and remain under their control, subject to registrar requirements. We explain account access and transfer terms before purchase.

What isn't included?

Premium plugins, paid AI agents, advanced SEO campaigns, paid advertising, online payments, custom databases, business email subscriptions and major changes are excluded unless quoted.

Can I subscribe instead of paying upfront?

A proposed Starter subscription is 12,000 RWF per month for a minimum 12-month agreement, subject to a separate written contract defining ownership, cancellation and renewal terms. It is an alternative to the one-time purchase, not an extra required charge.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. We can quote the website without a new domain purchase and connect an eligible domain you already own.

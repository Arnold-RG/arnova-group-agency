Know your future costs

What happens after the first year?

A website build does not include the domain forever. The first year covers an eligible .co.rw domain and static hosting; after that, a yearly renewal keeps the site online.

Indicative first-year domain upgrades and yearly renewal costs
Domain choice First-year domain upgrade Estimated yearly renewal (domain + hosting administration)
.co.rw Included 18,000 RWF (12,000 domain + 6,000 hosting administration)
.rw +9,000 RWF Confirm at quotation
.com +23,000 RWF 38,000 RWF (32,000 domain + 6,000 hosting administration)
.net +24,000 RWF Confirm at quotation
.org +23,000 RWF Confirm at quotation

Indicative prices only, not live domain availability or a registrar quote. Taxes, provider charges and non-static hosting can change the final price.

Common questions

No surprises after you order.

How do I start?

Send a short description of your business and what you want the website or digital tool to accomplish. We discuss requirements and confirm a written scope and quote before work starts.

What is included?

Starting prices for simple static business websites are listed on this page. Every package includes mobile-friendly design, contact links, and an eligible .co.rw domain and static hosting for the first year, subject to availability. Each card lists the rest of what that package includes.

Can I pay in EUR, USD or PLN?

Yes. Ask for a quote in EUR, USD or PLN. We confirm the exchange rate, the exact amount and the payment channel before you pay. Starting prices on this page are listed in Rwandan francs (RWF).

Do I have to pay every month?

No. Standard packages are one-time builds and maintenance is optional. Domain and hosting renewals apply after the first year.

Can I pay in two parts?

Yes, on request. The amounts, due dates and delivery conditions are written into your quote before work starts.

What if I need more pages or online payments?

We quote separately for extra pages, booking systems, databases, online checkout, integrations and other features outside your package.

Who owns my website?

Ownership, domain registration, access, third-party subscriptions and any transfer terms are set out in writing before you pay.

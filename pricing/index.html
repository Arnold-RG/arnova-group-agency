Payment made understandable

Choose how to pay for your website.

You do not have to buy a monthly maintenance plan. First choose how to pay for the build; then decide whether you want optional ongoing support.

1

One-time payment

Pay the agreed website package price once, with no mandatory monthly subscription. Annual domain and hosting renewals are billed separately after year one.

2

Two-stage payment

For eligible projects, request an agreed split such as 50% before work begins and 50% before launch. Example: a 65,000 RWF project becomes two payments of 32,500 RWF. Subject to approval.

3

Custom installment proposal

Need a different schedule? Ask us to quote one. We will state the total cost, due dates, ownership and cancellation terms in writing. No installment plan is automatic until approved.

How can you send payment?

Ask us which receiving channels are active before paying. Depending on availability, we may arrange MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, bank transfer, or a secure card/payment link. We confirm the verified payee details on your invoice.

These are possible channels, not a claim that every method is currently active. Never send funds to unverified details. We do not collect card information on this page.

Common questions

No surprises after you order.

Can I pay in euros, dollars or Polish złoty?

Yes, request a quote in EUR, USD or PLN. We confirm the exchange rate, exact invoice amount and available receiving channel before payment.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. Our standard website packages are one-time builds. Monthly maintenance is optional. Domain and hosting renewal costs apply when the first-year period ends.

Can I pay in two parts?

You can request an approved staged payment plan. We will write the amounts, due dates and delivery conditions in the quotation before starting work.

What if I need more pages or online payments?

We provide a custom quote for added pages, booking systems, databases, online checkout, integrations and other features outside the selected package.

Who owns my website?

Ownership, domain registration, access credentials, third-party subscriptions and any transfer arrangements are defined in your written agreement before payment.
